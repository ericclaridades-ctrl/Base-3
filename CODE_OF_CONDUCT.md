# Base-3 Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open, rigorous, and grounded scientific environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Base-3 project a harassment-free experience for everyone. 

We are building "The Good AI"—an engine dedicated to low-compute efficiency, ecological responsibility, and deterministic physical truth. We pledge to prioritize topological coherence and mathematical rigor over unconstrained scale. We are committed to building intelligence that course-corrects rather than hallucinates, and our community interactions must reflect that same standard of coherence and respect.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:**
*   **Mathematical and Architectural Rigor:** Grounding debates and pull requests in structural logic, spatial/temporal physics, and the epistemic triad.
*   **Ecological Mindfulness:** Proposing optimizations that reduce compute overhead and respect our low-energy mandate.
*   **Constructive Pushback:** Challenging the architecture or code with demonstrable edge cases, reproducible bugs, or formal proofs.
*   **Respect for the License:** Acknowledging and adhering to the AGPLv3 open-source obligations.
*   **Grace in Disagreement:** Keeping discussions focused on the technology, not the individual.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior include:**
*   **Ideological Trolling:** Bad-faith arguments or spamming the repository with demands to abandon determinism for probabilistic, scale-maximalist approaches. Constructive debate is welcome; dogmatic derailment is not.
*   **License Circumvention:** Harassing maintainers for permissive license downgrades (e.g., MIT/Apache) to bypass the AGPLv3 viral clause for proprietary closed-source integration. 
*   **Toxicity and Harassment:** The use of sexualized language, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, or any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional scientific setting.
*   **Willful Incoherence:** Repeatedly submitting PRs that intentionally bypass the triad's physical arbitration to force standard, unconstrained LLM behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue trackers, pull requests, and any official Base-3 community forums or mailing lists. It also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project founder and core maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, resulting in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. 

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Project maintainers will follow these community impact guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1.  **Correction:** For minor infractions (e.g., unprofessional language, failure to respect the license scope). A private or public warning with a request to correct the behavior.
2.  **Warning:** For sustained disruption, including repetitive bad-faith ideological arguments or minor harassment. A warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3.  **Temporary Ban:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication within the project for a specified period.
4.  **Permanent Ban:** For a pattern of violation, severe harassment, or intentional sabotage of the project's foundational goals. A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.
